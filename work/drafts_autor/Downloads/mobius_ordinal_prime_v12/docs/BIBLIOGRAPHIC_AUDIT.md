# Bibliographic audit and limits of comparison

The manuscript distinguishes classical theorems, deductions proved in this
chapter, conditional research targets, and numerical checks. The comparison
concerns the works listed here. No exhaustive priority search proves that
this construction differs from every other approach.

## The supplied survey

Alain Connes, *The Riemann Hypothesis: Past, Present and a Letter Through
Time*, arXiv:2602.04022v1, February 2026:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04022v1

The uploaded PDF was read directly. The relevant comparisons are:

| Survey location | Applicable statement | Limit of the proposed transfer |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1.3 | Guinand–Weil for admissible tests, with unnormalised Mellin transform | A regularised finite atomic measure gives a valid identity; its infinite limit still needs control. |
| 2.2.3 | Borchsenius–Jessen behaviour of zeta minus a nonzero constant | Pole stability under holomorphic addition is an elementary local fact; the full residual must first be holomorphic. |
| 2.2.4 | Voronin universality for fixed targets and tolerances | It does not rule out height-dependent polynomial lower bounds. An elementary lower bound for B gives a concrete countercheck. |
| 4.1 | Weil positivity, finite prime powers per compact test | Both vanishing Mellin moments and the archimedean term matter. A finite gap vector is not already an admissible function or the Weil form. |
| Introduction | Redheffer determinant formulation | The classical determinant identity is reproved. The new bordered prime-entry representation encodes T and SS but does not prove a growth bound. |
| 6.4–6.6 | A current truncated-Weil programme with explicit remaining steps | Simplicity/evenness of a lowest eigenfunction and comparison of approximating families remain distinct obligations. No operator route is pursued here. |

The survey is a map of results and programmes, not primary evidence that all
other approaches have failed. Its universal statements about individual
tests must retain their quantifiers over all windows and functions.

## Original construction versus classical ingredients

The particular encoding uses two sign-specific occurrence counters, ordinal
prime weights, an explicit nonsquarefree row, and exact pair/cut interfaces.
That identifies the object under study. Counting intervals, monotone
matching, Mellin transforms, Abel summation, bordered determinants and
positivity criteria have classical antecedents.

The full transformed sequence encodes the original sign sequence without
creating independent arithmetic input. A new proof mechanism would require
an independently justified estimate or identity at those interfaces. Its
existence, easier attainability, and universal novelty are not claimed.
