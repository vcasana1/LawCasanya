ORDINAL / HEIGHT / MULTIPLICITY AUDIT — v14
============================================

Primary convention
------------------
The zero positions are indexed by
  k = 1,2,...,N,
with N only the final value of k in a finite truncation.
There are never two equal values of k. What may repeat is the ordinate:
  gamma_k = gamma_{k+1} = ...
Each repeated occurrence occupies its own ordinal slot and shifts every later k.

Multiplicity accounting
-----------------------
1. Critical line:
   If 1/2+i gamma has algebraic multiplicity m, the positive ordinate gamma occupies
   m consecutive ordinal slots.

2. Off the critical line:
   If beta+i gamma, beta != 1/2, has multiplicity m, functional-equation and
   conjugation symmetry also give 1-beta+i gamma with multiplicity m. Hence the
   upper-half-plane enumeration contains 2m slots with the same positive ordinate
   gamma. The lower conjugates at -gamma are not indexed.

3. Coincident configurations:
   If several zero factors share the same positive ordinate, the block length is the
   total upper-half-plane algebraic multiplicity at that ordinate. All endpoint
   corrections use that total block length.

Why a height cutoff is not automatically the same as k=N
---------------------------------------------------------
A classical height sum at gamma_N includes every zero with ordinate <= gamma_N and
therefore the whole repeated-ordinate block at gamma_N. The ordinal sum through k=N
may stop inside that block. The difference is exactly the unused part of the one
terminal block. Therefore every external height theorem is used in this order:
  (i) evaluate the complete height sum at gamma_N;
  (ii) remove the unused terminal slots if N is inside a repeated block;
  (iii) convert the gamma_N scale to the N scale.
No formal substitution height=N is used.

Transport lemma
---------------
For every ordinal slot k, including slots inside a repeated-ordinate block,
  k = M(gamma_k) + O(log(2+gamma_k)),
so
  k asymp gamma_k log(2+gamma_k),
  gamma_k asymp k/log(2+k).
No RH or simplicity assumption is used.

External results and dependent statements
-----------------------------------------
A. Hlawka equidistribution.
   The complete-height result counts zeros with multiplicity. Evaluating at gamma_N
   and correcting only the unused terminal slots yields
     sum_{k<=N} exp(i gamma_k) = o(N).

B. Ford--Zaharescu second-order distribution.
   Precise input: Ford--Zaharescu (2005), Corollary 2, which gives the second-order
   formula for every C^2 test function. For f(u)=exp(2*pi*i*u) and
   alpha=1/(2*pi), the mean vanishes and the secondary density g_alpha is identically
   zero because alpha is non-resonant. Thus the COMPLETE height sum satisfies
     sum_{0<gamma<=gamma_N} exp(i gamma) = o(gamma_N).
   Correcting the possibly partial terminal block by O(nu(gamma_N)) and then using
   gamma_N asymp N/log N gives
     sum_{k<=N} exp(i gamma_k) = o(N/log N).
   Ordinary equidistribution alone would only give o(N) on the ordinal scale and is
   insufficient for the stronger rate.

C. Level 1.
   The Stieltjes argument is first carried out over complete ordinate blocks.
   For an arbitrary N, completing the terminal block changes the ordinal-defect sum
   by O(log^2(2+gamma_N)). This remains o(N), including critical-line multiplicity
   and off-line repeated pairs. The Ford--Zaharescu input used inside the proof is
   exactly item B above.

D. H versus M clock replacement.
   This step is already ordinal:
     sum_{k<=N}(r_k^(M)-r_k)exp(i gamma_k)=O(log^2(2+gamma_N))=o(N).
   Repeated ordinates are counted slot by slot. No extra height-to-index assumption is
   introduced beyond the transport lemma.

E. True-zero M-weighted statistic.
   The needed B_N=o(N/log N) is item B with the terminal-block correction. Abel
   summation is then performed directly in the ordinal variable k and gives
     sum_{k<=N} k exp(i gamma_k)=o(N^2/log N).
   The identity M(gamma_k)=k+O(log(2+gamma_k)) remains valid inside repeated blocks.

F. Selberg second moment.
   At the endpoint height gamma_N,
     integral_0^{gamma_N} S(t)^2 dt << gamma_N log log gamma_N.
   Dividing by N and using the transport lemma gives o(1). If N lies inside a
   repeated block, gamma_N is unchanged while N differs from the complete block index
   by only O(nu(gamma_N)); this does not alter the asymptotic conversion.

G. Uniform Landau/Gonek input under RH.
   The classical formula is applied to the complete block at gamma_N. Under RH an
   algebraic multiplicity-m zero still contributes m equal terms. The possible partial
   terminal block contributes O(nu(gamma_N)), after which the result is expressed in N.
   Off-line blocks do not arise in this RH-conditional step.

H. Fujii horizontal moment.
   A_2 is deliberately kept as a complete-height quantity. No partial ordinal A_2 is
   defined inside a common-ordinate block, because off-line zeros at the same gamma can
   have different beta values and their within-block order is arbitrary.

I. Holomorphic centroid, Theorem 10.1.
   Both terms in C_N^zeta(u)-C^zeta(u) are now explicitly bounded. The finite weighted
   term is <= N^{-1} sum_{k>=1} k exp(-y gamma_k), and the tail is bounded by the same
   convergent majorant. Repeated ordinates are separate k-slots and are included in the
   majorant with their multiplicity.

J. Completed-zero defect and RH criterion.
   A critical-line multiplicity m contributes equally to C^zeta and C^xi and hence zero
   defect. An off-line pair beta+i gamma and 1-beta+i gamma of multiplicity m contributes
   m times the paired nonnegative defect. The infinite absolutely convergent series is
   independent of arbitrary within-block ordering.

K. RVM residual corollary (formerly Theorem 10.4).
   R_RVM-A_RVM=D identically, so the statement is now a Corollary and its proof is
   immediate from the preceding RH-equivalence theorem. If RH fails, repeated positive
   ordinates insert additional ordinal slots and therefore shift all subsequent tau_k.
   Thus gamma_k-tau_k is always the exact ordinally paired Lambert residual, but off RH
   it is not the displacement obtained by freezing a hypothetical RH enumeration.
