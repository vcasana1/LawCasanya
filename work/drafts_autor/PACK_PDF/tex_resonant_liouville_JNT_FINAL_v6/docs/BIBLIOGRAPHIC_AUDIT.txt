Bibliographic audit for the final manuscript

- N. Frantzikinakis, arXiv:1908.02732: Correlations of multiplicative functions along deterministic and independent sequences.
- D. Crncevic, F. Hernandez, K. Rizk, K. Sereesuchart, R. Tao, arXiv:2211.15830: On the multiplicative independence between n and floor(alpha n); Liouville result is for irrational alpha.
- J. Teravainen, A. Walker, arXiv:2303.12574 / Math. Z. 310 (2025), Art. 75: Theorem 1.2 treats two-point Beatty correlations; in the rationally dependent case the logarithmic limit exists and the theorem characterizes when it vanishes.
- The manuscript derives the explicit non-vanishing rational value lambda(pq)/q from the residue-class decomposition and Tao's affine logarithmic cancellation; it does not attribute that explicit value to the cited TW theorem statement.
